Privacy Policy

Hermes Agent · last updated 16 October 2026

What this app is

Hermes Agent is a self-hosted, single-user automation assistant operated by its owner for personal use. It is not offered to the public. The only Google account it connects to is a dedicated account owned by the operator.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, the app may access, for that one account:

  • Gmail: reading, labeling, and sending messages.
  • Google Drive, Docs, and Sheets: reading, creating, and editing files the account can access.
  • Google Calendar and Contacts: reading and managing events and reading contacts.

How the data is used

Data is used only to carry out tasks the owner asks the assistant to perform, such as summarizing email, reading shared documents, or drafting replies. Message and document content may be sent to an AI model provider to generate those results. Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with any other third party.

Storage and retention

OAuth tokens and any cached data are stored on a private server controlled by the owner. No data is stored by anyone other than the owner. The owner can delete stored data at any time by removing it from that server.

Limited Use disclosure

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's third-party access settings. Doing so invalidates the stored token immediately.
